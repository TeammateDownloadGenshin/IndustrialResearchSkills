# Source and Citation Policy

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. regulators, exchanges, governments, courts, statutory filings, official trial registries, and official labels;
2. company filings, investor-relations documents, official protocols, and original transaction documents;
3. peer-reviewed primary studies, recognized standards bodies, original databases, patent records, and industry associations;
4. reputable news or press-release distribution for time-sensitive context that cannot yet be obtained from a stronger source.

Use aggregators, portals, social media, newsletters, podcasts, search snippets, AI summaries, and broker research only to discover leads or represent attributed opinion.

## Time validity

When recreating or auditing a report, accept only evidence publicly available by the report cutoff. Record both publication and access dates. If the source date is uncertain, mark it unresolved rather than silently treating the current page as historical proof.

For a current report, use the latest available source while preserving the underlying data cutoff. A newly published source may still report old data.

Maintain a freshness ledger for material sources and datasets: `as_of`, publication date, access date, reporting period or clinical data cutoff, refresh trigger, and supersession status. Label material as `current`, `historical`, `superseded`, or `needs_refresh`. Never infer current status from file modification time alone.

## Direct support

A citation must visibly support the mapped claim. A source homepage, broad company description, or search snippet does not support a specific number, date, product status, endpoint, market share, or transaction term.

Label evidence as:

- `direct`: explicitly states the fact;
- `derived`: supplies all inputs to a transparent calculation;
- `partial`: supports only part of the claim;
- `context`: informs interpretation but does not prove the material claim.

## Conflict handling

Preserve conflicting values until scope, period, unit, currency, revision status, consolidation basis, trial population, data cutoff, or definition explains the difference. Record the rejected value and the reason for choosing another.

## Access and privacy

Do not bypass access controls or use credentials/API keys without explicit user authorization. Do not upload confidential reports, patient-level data, material nonpublic information, or licensed content to external services without authorization. Minimize quotations and honor applicable licenses and copyright.
