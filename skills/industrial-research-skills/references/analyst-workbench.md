# Healthcare Analyst Workbench

Use the smallest module that matches the deliverable. These modules reflect repeated, validated work patterns in the local healthcare research archive; they are workflows, not reusable factual claims.

## Earnings note and rolling workpaper

Update official-period financials, segment/product splits, quarterly revenue and profit, margins, expense ratios, R&D, cash flow, working capital, and operational indicators. Preserve reported versus adjusted measures, cumulative versus single-quarter semantics, units, scope, and prior-period comparability. Use formulas for derived quarters, growth, margins, and ratios. Deliver an updated XLSX workpaper, concise variance explanation, and a Word-ready conclusion; do not overwrite the prior-period file.

## Company deep dive

Build the investment argument from company history and ownership through industry chain, products, capacity, customers, competition, economics, forecasts, valuation, catalysts, and risks. Each figure or table needs a source note and corresponding workpaper. Treat a title such as “cycle recovery”, “capacity release”, or “platform value” as a hypothesis to test with volume, price, utilization, mix, margins, cash conversion, and counter-evidence.

## Biomedical asset and platform diligence

Separate target biology, modality design, translational rationale, clinical evidence, regulatory status, CMC/manufacturing, competitive position, commercial opportunity, partnership economics, and valuation. Maintain:

- a program table by asset, indication, biomarker, phase, trial ID, design, endpoint, population, enrollment, data cutoff, and next milestone;
- a competitor table reconciled for line of therapy, eligibility, comparator, follow-up, efficacy definition, and safety exposure;
- a mechanism chain that labels human clinical evidence, preclinical evidence, company assertion, and analyst inference;
- a catalyst ledger whose dates are sourced and whose success/failure conditions are explicit.

For target-expression figures, preserve cohort definition, sample level versus patient level, statistic, transformation, missing-data treatment, and limitations. RNA, protein, membrane localization, co-expression, internalization, and drug sensitivity are distinct claims.

## Thematic and technology research

For ADCs, TCEs, molecular glues, mRNA, antibodies, APIs, formulations, or manufacturing platforms, combine a development timeline, mechanism diagram, value-chain map, representative assets, technical bottlenecks, clinical or industrial validation, and investable company mapping. Do not let a mechanism diagram imply clinical validation or platform-wide transferability.

## MNC and peer-quarter comparison

Normalize reporting currency, period, company fiscal calendars, product definitions, acquisitions/divestitures, and constant-currency versus reported growth. Separate company sheets from the cross-company summary. Trace every product or pipeline number to the relevant filing, results presentation, or transcript page. Use the same comparison fields across peers and retain a quality-control response log.

## Catalyst and event review

Map regulatory, clinical, BD/licensing, commercial, policy, and operating events to dated evidence. A price inflection near an event is association, not proof of causality. If the deliverable claims market impact, use a defined benchmark and event window and record alternative explanations.

## Weekly sector report

Maintain a dated queue for sector events, company disclosures, scientific/regulatory milestones, shareholder meetings, and the team's published research. Promote only decision-relevant items into the narrative; attach the original source and avoid recycling an old weekly conclusion after its catalyst window has passed.

## Figure and document delivery

For every figure, retain source data, calculation code or formulas, title, units, date cutoff, source note, and limitations. Follow the current team template for colors, fonts, and dimensions; template examples are not factual sources. Render and visually inspect final XLSX, DOCX, PPTX, and PDF outputs. Check labels, legends, page breaks, chart ranges, table wrapping, hyperlinks, and whether the displayed figure matches the saved source data.

## Completion gate

Before delivery, confirm: conclusions lead; facts, calculations, assumptions, and interpretations are distinguishable; material claims map to time-valid sources; stale or superseded evidence is labeled; unresolved items remain visible; and the final artifact has been rendered or otherwise visually checked.
